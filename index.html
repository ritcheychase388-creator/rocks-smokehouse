Locally Owned & Family Operated

From A Topeka Food Truck To Downtown Quincy Street

Owned and operated by Richard and Jasmaine Harris, J Rocks Smoke House started with a simple mission: bring authentic, slow-smoked barbecue and real-deal soul food to the Topeka community.

After two years of serving loyal fans out of our food truck on SE 21st Street—rain, shine, or Kansas wind—we achieved our dream of opening our very first brick-and-mortar restaurant right in the heart of Downtown Topeka at 1003 SE Quincy Street.
